How to register: Grab, Angkas, Joyride

Check before you apply. Requirements, fees and vehicle rules are set by each company and by the LTFRB, and they change often. This is a general guide, not official advice. Always confirm in the company's official app or website. These apps have their own vehicle rules, so a tricycle may not qualify for every service.

Grab (GrabCar / TNVS)

Grab car drivers operate as a Transport Network Vehicle Service (TNVS), which needs LTFRB authority (a Provisional Authority, then a Certificate of Public Convenience).

Documents usually needed

  • Valid government ID and proof of Filipino citizenship
  • LTO OR/CR of the vehicle (in your name, or the owner's authorization)
  • Professional driver's license
  • NBI clearance and police clearance
  • Passenger insurance
  • Garage proof and a Certificate of Conformity if the car is financed (outside Metro Manila)

Steps

  1. Prepare your documents and check the vehicle rules in the Grab Driver app or website.
  2. Submit your requirements to a Grab Driver Center (Metro Manila, or Cebu, Pampanga and Bacolod for other areas).
  3. Wait for the Provisional Authority (PA). Grab sends an SMS and the wait can take weeks to months.
  4. Get activated at a Grab Driver Center once you have your case number and PA.
  5. Attend the online LTFRB hearing for your CPC.
  6. Once the CPC is approved, have the vehicle reclassified as a public utility vehicle at the LTO.
  7. Renew the CPC before it expires.

Angkas (motorcycle taxi)

Angkas bikers apply online, then complete an in-person assessment and training before they can take rides.

Documents usually needed

Based on a third-party guide, so confirm the current list with Angkas.

  • Age 21 to 55
  • Valid professional driver's license
  • Motorcycle OR and CR (valid), from an accepted brand and recent model year
  • NBI clearance and police or barangay clearance
  • Medical certificate if you are in the older age bracket
  • Authorization letter or deed of sale if you are not the registered owner
  • A smartphone that meets the app's requirements

Steps

  1. Scan or photograph all your documents.
  2. Open the Angkas application page and upload your requirements.
  3. Fill in your personal, motorcycle and license details, then agree to the declarations and sign up.
  4. Wait for a call or text about visiting an Angkas office.
  5. Bring your original documents for processing and the skills assessment (obstacle course and road signs test).
  6. Attend the safety training and the Angkas app training.
  7. Start accepting rides.

Joyride

Joyride is a Philippine ride-hailing service that started with motorcycle rides and now also offers JoyRide Car. For its car service it has held onboarding events for qualified TNVS drivers.

Documents usually needed

We could not confirm Joyride's current official requirements, so these are the documents ride-hailing platforms in the Philippines commonly ask for. Confirm the exact list with Joyride.

  • Valid government ID
  • Driver's license (professional license for most services)
  • LTO OR/CR of your vehicle
  • NBI and/or police clearance
  • For car service: LTFRB TNVS authority

Steps

  1. Check Joyride's official website or app for the driver or rider sign-up page and the current requirements.
  2. Submit your details and upload your documents.
  3. Attend onboarding or orientation if Joyride asks for it. Some onboarding events offer sign-up incentives.
  4. Install the partner app, set up your profile and start accepting rides.
