Notifications & Sound

SMS notifications Get a text when your driver accepts, is arriving, starts and finishes your trip.
Sound Play a tone for new ride requests, driver found, arrival, new messages and trip completed.
Vibration Vibrate with each alert (on phones that support it).
Phone notifications Show alerts while the app is in the background.

SMS messages are sent to the mobile number saved in Edit Profile. Standard SMS rates from your carrier may apply when you reply.
